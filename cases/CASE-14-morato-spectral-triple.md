**What the symmetry is.** An earlier revision framed the concern as
guilt-by-association -- the Millennium-Problem claims read as a reputational
hazard to the wider H4-to-SM programme, the lab's own included. That framing
was about the lab's interests rather than the subject's claims, carried no
weight in any label, and was removed under CHARTER.md s 1 and s 4. What carries
weight is that the lab and the subject work the same geometric object in the
same formalism, and that on the shared claim M1 the two sit in the same label
for the same two reasons. The lab's advantage is its ledger and its stated
Fpaths elsewhere, not its derivation on this object.

the lab's own -- under one matched-cardinality control alphabet replacing phi
with (e, pi, sqrt(2), sqrt(3), small integers), scored under BH-FDR at q=0.05
against a target list fixed before the fit. Collapse under the control moves
the phi-specific reading toward [Retracted] for whichever programme collapses;
survival promotes it from [Risk] to [Open conjecture] -- for either, or both.
