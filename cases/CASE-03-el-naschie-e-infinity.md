## 1. Identity

Mohamed El Naschie's early work (1974-1990) concerned structural mechanics and
the mathematics of buckling in mainstream applied-mathematics journals. From the
early 1990s he developed the E-infinity programme: quantum spacetime is an infinite-
dimensional hierarchical random Cantor set whose effective Hausdorff dimension is
4 + phi^(-3) = 4.2360679..., where phi = (1 + sqrt(5))/2 is the golden ratio. The
programme further claims to derive Standard Model coupling constants, dark energy
density, and Newton's gravitational constant from phi expressions.

El Naschie founded *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals* in 1991 and served as editor-in-
chief until 2009, publishing over 300 of his own papers there. The overwhelming
majority of his post-1990 publications appear in that journal and, after 2009, in
Scientific Research Publishing (SCIRP) family journals. No E-infinity paper has
been published in a peer-reviewed mainstream physics journal (Physical Review,
Nuclear Physics B, Communications in Mathematical Physics) under independent
editorial control.

Leila Marek-Crnjac (Technical School Centre of Maribor, Slovenia; catalog entry
#07) is the active continuator of the programme, publishing co-authored and
independent papers exclusively in SCIRP venues through at least 2013.

## 4. [Verified] inventory

- **[Verified]** The arithmetic identity 4 + phi^(-3) = 4.2360679... is correct.
  phi^(-1) = (sqrt(5)-1)/2 = 0.6180339...; phi^(-3) = (phi^(-1))^3 = 0.2360679...;
  sum = 4.2360679... Source: elementary algebra. Evidence: exact-by-construction,
  re-derivable in two lines from the definition phi^2 = phi + 1.

- **[Verified]** The Mauldin-Williams (1986) theorem establishes that for a random
  recursive construction satisfying the natural open-set condition, an almost-sure
  universal Hausdorff dimension alpha exists, determined by an expected-value equation
  generalising Moran's formula. Source: Mauldin and Williams (1986), *Trans. AMS*
  295(1), https://www.ams.org/journals/tran/1986-295-01/S0002-9947-1986-0831202-5/.
  Evidence: published proof in a peer-reviewed AMS journal; the result is
  mathematically established and independent of E-infinity.

- **[Verified]** The application of Mauldin-Williams to a random Cantor construction
  where each building block has Hausdorff dimension phi^(-1) is a legitimate instance
  of the theorem. The mathematical machinery is standard. This is [Verified] as an
  application of established mathematics; it is separately [Risk] as a claim about
  physical spacetime (Section 7).

## 5. [Empirical fit] inventory

No E-infinity claim survives [Empirical fit] classification. The programme presents
physical-constant agreements as first-principles derivations, not as fits to data.
No chi^2 comparison, no statement of free parameters, and no pre-registered held-out
test appear in the published record. Claims presented as derivations but lacking an
independent derivation path are classified under [Risk] / [High-risk] per the
framework. An [Empirical fit] label would require the programme to acknowledge that
the numerical agreements are post-hoc, which the programme does not do.

---

## 6. [Open conjecture] inventory

No E-infinity claim survives [Open conjecture] classification. For this label, a
claim must carry a stated falsification path (Fpath): a specific statement of the
form "this claim is falsified if X is observed." The E-infinity programme states
no such conditions for any of its major claims. The dimensional claim
(4 + phi^(-3) = Hausdorff dimension of quantum spacetime) aspires to [Open
conjecture] -- it is a specific numerical prediction about physical spacetime --
but without a stated Fpath it falls to [Risk] (a) per the framework's most
important rule.

## 7. [Risk] / [High-risk] inventory

- **[Risk]** The claim that quantum spacetime has Hausdorff dimension
  4 + phi^(-3) = 4.2360679... Source:
  https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=39841.
  Reason: (a) No falsification path stated. The claim is a specific numerical
  prediction about physical spacetime and could in principle be tested against
  quantum gravity phenomenology (Planck-scale dispersion relations in gamma-ray
  burst timing, or a theoretical derivation within an independent framework such
  as loop quantum gravity or causal set theory). The programme identifies none of
  these as what would falsify the claim. Without a stated Fpath, [Open conjecture]
  is not available.

- **[High-risk]** The claim that Standard Model coupling constants and Newton's
  gravitational constant are all derivable from phi "from first principles."
  Source: Helal, Marek-Crnjac, He (2013),
  https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=39841.
  Reason: (a) No Fpath stated. (b) Look-elsewhere correction absent: any real
  number can be approximated to arbitrary precision by phi-expressions; the space
  of such expressions is effectively unbounded, and no correction for the number
  of expressions tried is reported. (d) Venue weakness: all post-2009 papers
  asserting these derivations appear exclusively in SCIRP family journals with no
  independent editorial control established for these specific claims.
  [High-risk] because criteria (a), (b), and (d) are simultaneously satisfied.

- **[High-risk]** The claim that dark energy density agrees "completely with
  measurements" via a phi-based formula. Source: same survey.
  Reason: (a) no Fpath, (b) unbounded look-elsewhere, (d) SCIRP venue.
  Additionally, "agrees completely" without specifying the measurement source or
  precision is not an auditable statement.

- **[Risk]** Venue and reproducibility record. El Naschie published over 300 papers
  as editor-in-chief of *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals*. The *Scholarly Kitchen* (2008)
  initially reported 82% self-citation; a correction posted 2009-02-12 revised this
  to approximately 32% (263/821 citing articles were self-citations; the original
  82% figure resulted from a methodological error). Source:
  https://scholarlykitchen.sspnet.org/2008/11/25/elsevier-math-editor-controversy/.
  **The corrected figure is ~32%; the 82% figure must not be cited.**
  Separately, the UK High Court found an "implausible absence of documentation"
  of peer review and that El Naschie "had failed to provide any documentary
  evidence that his papers had been peer-reviewed": [2012] EWHC 1809 (QB),
  https://inforrm.org/2012/07/18/case-law-el-naschie-v-macmillan-claim-against-
  scientific-publisher-dismissed-gervase-de-wilde/.
  This [Risk] label applies to the evidential weight of the publication record as
  independent verification, not to the mathematical content of the claims.
  Reason: (d) venue weakness -- the court finding establishes that the publication
  channel lacked the evidentiary properties of independent peer review.

- **[High-risk]** Independent reproduction absent. As of 2026-06-02, no paper in
  a mainstream physics journal with fully independent authorship (no author shared
  with El Naschie, Marek-Crnjac, He, Olsen, Helal, or Nada) has been identified
  that independently derives and empirically tests an E-infinity prediction. The
  post-2009 continuation (Marek-Crnjac et al.) remains within the same co-author
  network, all publishing in SCIRP venues. Reason: (b) no independent look-elsewhere
  control, (d) no independent venue. [High-risk] (b, d).

| 300+ papers in editor-controlled journal; court finding of "implausible absence of documentation" of peer review | IGLA RACE v2 BPB-per-format table (gf16 BPB 2.5725 vs bf16 2.6135) circulated as evidence, unreproducible from public HEAD fab7d81 | Both venue/reproducibility failures. Ours was caught internally and demoted to [CLAIM, NEEDS REPRODUCTION] (CASE-00 s 7). His was caught by *Nature* and confirmed by the court. |

The first symmetry is the most instructive. The lab's own delta_CP = 3/phi^2 claim
was a phi-anchored prediction about a measured physical constant, exactly parallel
to E-infinity's phi^(-3)-anchored dimensional claim. Both have the form: [number
derivable from phi] = [physically meaningful parameter]. The lab retracted its
claim after an independent arithmetic check showed the numerical match failed at
required precision (CASE-00 s 8). El Naschie's programme has not retracted the
analogous claim and carries no stated Fpath that would make retraction possible
in principle. This is the structural asymmetry: we acknowledged the look-elsewhere
problem and withdrew; the E-infinity programme has not.

The second symmetry is the algebra-versus-physics boundary: both programmes anchor
in a [Verified] algebraic identity (4 + phi^(-3) = 4.236... and phi^2 + phi^(-2)
= 3, respectively) and then make an additional, unverified physical-interpretation
claim. The algebra is [Verified] in both cases. The physical claim is not.

The joint Fpath that would resolve both sides at once: state in advance what
experimental outcome falsifies the phi-derived value of the physical quantity in
question, then obtain that measurement from an independent experimental group.
Neither side has done this. The lab has the advantage of a retraction; E-infinity
has the advantage of mathematical precision in its target quantity.

## 10. Audit summary

**Strongest part:** The algebraic identities involving phi -- specifically that
4 + phi^(-3) = 4.2360679... is correct arithmetic, and that the Mauldin-Williams
(1986) theorem provides a legitimate framework for Hausdorff dimensions of random
recursive Cantor constructions -- are [Verified]. The mathematical skeleton of
E-infinity draws on real and established mathematics, and this credit is recorded
first per CHARTER.md s 1.

**Weakest claim:** The derivation of Standard Model coupling constants from phi
expressions is [High-risk] (a, b, d simultaneously): no falsification path, no
look-elsewhere correction over an effectively unbounded search space of phi-
expressions, and publication exclusively in venues where the court confirmed an
"implausible absence of documentation" of independent peer review.

**Single experiment that would move the largest claim:** State a specific,
actionable falsification condition for the claim that quantum spacetime has Hausdorff
dimension 4 + phi^(-3) and not some other number (e.g., specify what Planck-scale
dispersion measurement, or what result from loop quantum gravity or causal set
theory, would count as falsification). This Fpath, if stated and actionable,
promotes the dimensional claim from [Risk] (a) to [Open conjecture]. Subsequent
independent measurement confirmation could then promote it further.

**Symmetric position of the lab:** The lab's analogous phi-as-physical-constant
claim (delta_CP = 3/phi^2) was [Retracted] when the arithmetic check failed. The
lab's [Verified] anchors (GF ladder arithmetic, GF16 FPGA result) survive. Open
conjectures carry stated Fpaths and are compute-pending. The contrast -- [Verified]
algebra, [Risk] physics claims, no stated Fpaths, no retractions -- is the
structural difference between the two programmes.
