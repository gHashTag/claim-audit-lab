## 1. Identity

Tejinder P. Singh is a senior professor at TIFR Mumbai, formerly long-affiliated with IUCAA Pune, sitting on the editorial board of *Foundations of Physics* (Springer). His core programme is **trace dynamics** -- an extension of classical mechanics into non-commutative matrix-valued variables that recovers both QM and GR as thermodynamic limits.

In a sequence of preprints from 2020 onward, Singh has incorporated **octonion** and **Clifford-algebra** structures and reported derivations of Standard Model fermion mass ratios and coupling constants in which phi-arithmetic expressions appear. Unlike fringe authors, his work is co-authored, indexed in INSPIRE-HEP, and occasionally cited.

Primary references for this case (all URLs fetched on 2026-06-16):

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16274 -- "Trace dynamics, and a ground-state in spontaneous quantum gravity" (2020)
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.05574 -- "An octonionic theory of unification" (2020)
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14344 -- "Why does the Kerr-Newman black hole have the same gyromagnetic ratio as the electron?" (2021)
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06614 -- "The characteristic equation of the exceptional Jordan algebra: its eigenvalues, and their relation to mass ratios for quarks and leptons" (2022)
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18139 -- "From trace dynamics to a unified theory of fundamental interactions" (2025)
