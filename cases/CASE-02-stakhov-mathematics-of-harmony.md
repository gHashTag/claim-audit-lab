## 1. Identity

Alexey Stakhov is a Ukrainian-Canadian mathematician who has spent four
decades developing a programme he calls "Mathematics of Harmony." The central
thesis is that Euclid's *Elements* is the source of two independent
mathematical directions: Classical Mathematics (axiomatic approach) and
Harmony Mathematics (golden section and Platonic solids). The programme spans
number theory (Fibonacci p-numbers, golden p-proportions, a new constructive
theory of real numbers), function theory (hyperbolic Fibonacci and Lucas
functions), computer science (Fibonacci computers, ternary mirror-symmetrical
arithmetic, error-correcting codes), and theoretical physics (hyperbolic
models of nature, "golden" interpretation of special relativity).

The formal academic anchor for the programme is a pair of World Scientific
monograph series. The 2009 single-volume work (*The Mathematics of Harmony:
From Euclid to Contemporary Mathematics and Computer Science*, Series on Knots
and Everything, Vol. 22) provides the foundational exposition. The 2020-2021
three-volume expansion (*Mathematics of Harmony as a New Interdisciplinary
Direction and "Golden" Paradigm of Modern Science*, World Scientific, Knots
and Everything series, Vols. 1-3) is the most formally developed statement of
the programme.

## 3. Tier mapping

Stakhov does not use an explicit epistemic tier system within his published
work. He uses rhetorical gradations (theorem, application, conjecture,
"fundamental suggestion") but these are not formalised into a taxonomy with
stated falsification conditions. The case file therefore applies the most
permissive reading consistent with the subject's strongest wording, per
FRAMEWORK.md s "No taxonomy stated."

The closest thing to a stated uncertainty in the programme is the
"Fundamental Suggestion" (p. 27 of the peacefromharmony preprint, quoted above
in Section 2), which is framed as a conditional: "If ... then ...". This framing
acknowledges the claim is not yet established; however, no falsification path is
articulated. This defaults to [Risk] (a) under our framework.

| Stakhov's implicit label | Our label | Reason |
|--------------------------|-----------|--------|
| Proved theorem (algebraic identity) | [Verified] | Exact-by-construction |
| "Published result" with stated algebraic proof | [Verified] | Re-derivable |
| Applied application to computer arithmetic | [Empirical fit] or [Risk] | Depends on whether hardware exists |
| "Important discovery" / "main idea" (no Fpath) | [Risk] (a) | No Fpath stated |
| "Fundamental Suggestion" | [Risk] (a) | Conditional framing but no falsifier |

## 4. [Verified] inventory

Claims from Stakhov's work that survive [Verified] under our framework. These
are the mathematical results that can be re-derived from definitions.

- **[Verified]** Fibonacci p-numbers satisfy a generalised recurrence
  \(F_p(n) = F_p(n-1) + F_p(n-p-1)\), with the ordinary Fibonacci sequence as
  the case p = 1. Source: *The Mathematics of Harmony* (World Scientific, 2009),
  https://www.worldscientific.com/worldscibooks/10.1142/6635. Evidence:
  algebraic identity derivable from the definition of the recurrence; the
  family is internally consistent and the special case reduces to the classical
  Fibonacci sequence.

- **[Verified]** The Q-matrix identity: the 2x2 matrix
  \(Q = [[1,1],[1,0]]\) raised to the n-th power equals
  \(Q^n = [[F(n+1), F(n)],[F(n), F(n-1)]]\), from which the Cassini identity
  \(F(n+1)F(n-1) - F(n)^2 = (-1)^n\) follows by taking determinants. Source:
  *The Mathematics of Harmony* (2009), same URL. Evidence: elementary matrix
  algebra, determinant of a product identity; standard result in Fibonacci
  literature.

- **[Verified]** The symmetric hyperbolic Fibonacci and Lucas functions (sFs,
  sLs) defined by Stakhov and Rosin satisfy analogues of the standard
  hyperbolic identities (hyperbolic Pythagorean theorem variant) and, at
  integer arguments, reduce to the ordinary Fibonacci and Lucas numbers.
  Source: Stakhov & Aranson (2010), *Applied Mathematics* (SCIRP),
  https://www.scirp.org/pdf/AM20100100019_19605174.pdf. Evidence: the
  reduction to integer sequences at discrete values is an algebraic identity
  that can be verified by direct substitution; the Binet-formula connection is
  standard.

- **[Verified]** Codes of the golden p-proportions (Bergman's number system
  extended to p >= 1): every natural number has a finite representation in the
  Fibonacci p-code (i.e., as a finite sum of Fibonacci p-numbers with binary
  coefficients). Source: Stakhov (2007), *Chaos, Solitons and Fractals*, 33,
  https://fs.unm.edu/SN/TheGeneralizedGolden.pdf. Evidence: Theorem 1 in the
  paper; the proof is a constructive algorithm derivable from the definition of
  Fibonacci p-numbers and the greedy representation.

- **[Verified]** The ternary mirror-symmetrical number system (base tau, the
  tribonacci ratio, with symmetric digit set) has a relative code redundancy of
  1.283 (i.e., approximately 28.3% more digits than binary for the same range).
  Source: same paper, same URL. Evidence: information-theoretic calculation
  from the digit set size and log base; re-derivable in two lines.

- **[Verified]** 65 patents in the US, Japan, England, France, Germany, Canada,
  and other countries were filed during 1976-1991 on Fibonacci computer
  concepts. Source: Stakhov, "Clarifying the Origins," p. 22,
  http://peacefromharmony.org/docs/7-27_Stakhov_Math_of_Harmony_EN.pdf.
  Evidence: patent records are public documents; existence of patent filings is
  a factual matter independent of whether the hardware was built or was
  effective. This is [Verified] as a fact about intellectual property records,
  NOT as a demonstration of working hardware.

## 5. [Empirical fit] inventory

- **[Empirical fit]** Phyllotaxis patterns (Fibonacci numbers of spirals in
  pine cones, sunflowers, pineapples) correspond numerically to consecutive
  Fibonacci numbers. Source: *The Mathematics of Harmony* (2009),
  https://www.worldscientific.com/worldscibooks/10.1142/6635; Stakhov &
  Aranson (2010), https://www.scirp.org/pdf/AM20100100019_19605174.pdf. Free
  parameters: the selection of which biological structures to measure and which
  Fibonacci pairs to match (the measurement protocol is post-hoc in the absence
  of a pre-registered specimen-selection rule). Control: the Douady-Couder
  (1992) mechanistic model explains the same spiral counts from a purely
  physical self-organisation process without requiring phi as input (see
  Section 6 for the Bodnar sub-claim). Pre-registered held-out test: absent.
  This is [Empirical fit] for "Fibonacci numbers appear in phyllotaxis counts";
  it is NOT [Verified] for "phi is the necessary geometric origin of
  phyllotaxis" (see [Risk] inventory).

## 6. [Open conjecture] inventory

- **[Open conjecture]** Bodnar's geometry (sub-claim of the Stakhov school):
  phyllotaxis is a geometric necessity derivable from hyperbolic Fibonacci
  functions, meaning the golden mean is the generative constant of biological
  growth geometry -- not merely an observed ratio but the causal geometric
  structure. Source: Stakhov & Aranson (2010), *Applied Mathematics*,
  https://www.scirp.org/pdf/AM20100100019_19605174.pdf; catalog entry #12
  (Bodnar) in phi_theorists_catalog.md. **Fpath (stated by subject):** No
  explicit falsification path is stated for the causal claim. However, the
  claim is in principle falsifiable by comparing the predictive scope of
  Bodnar's hyperbolic geometry against the established mechanistic control
  (Douady & Couder, 1992, *Physical Review Letters* 68, 2098:
  https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.68.2098). The Douady-Couder
  model derives Fibonacci phyllotaxis from a physical self-organisation
  process (a system's trend to avoid rational/periodic organisation converges
  to the golden mean as a dynamical attractor) without presupposing phi as a
  geometric input. If Bodnar's geometry is a re-description of the same
  dynamical output, the claim that phi is the necessary geometric origin is
  [Open conjecture] pending a test that distinguishes causal geometry from
  convergent approximation. Executable: the comparison is well-defined --
  both models predict Fibonacci spiral counts; the distinguishing question
  is whether the hyperbolic geometric structure gives any prediction the
  Douady-Couder model does not. This has not been addressed in published
  form within the Stakhov school. **Note on Fpath ownership:** the subject
  has not stated this falsification path; it is supplied by the lab in good
  faith. Without the subject's explicit Fpath this claim borders [Risk] (a);
  it is held at [Open conjecture] because the structure of the claim is such
  that the test is straightforwardly constructible.

- **[Open conjecture]** A "golden" non-Euclidean geometry based on hyperbolic
  Fibonacci functions constitutes an original solution to Hilbert's Fourth
  Problem (the problem of specifying all geometries whose straight lines are
  the shortest paths between points). Source: Stakhov & Aranson (2010),
  https://www.scirp.org/pdf/AM20100100019_19605174.pdf; Stakhov & Aranson
  (2016), *The "Golden" Non-Euclidean Geometry* (World Scientific), referenced
  in Stakhov (2016), https://periodicals.karazin.ua/cscs/article/view/6222.
  **Fpath (stated by subject):** Not explicitly stated. The solution to
  Hilbert's Fourth Problem was officially completed by Busemann and Pogorelov
  in the 20th century; whether the hyperbolic Fibonacci geometry constitutes
  an original contribution within that solved framework or reopens the problem
  is a mathematical question that mainstream geometry journals have not
  adjudicated. Executable: submission to and decision by a peer-reviewed
  geometry journal (not SCIRP) would constitute evidence.

## 7. [Risk] / [High-risk] inventory

- **[Risk]** "Harmony Mathematics" as a parallel mathematical direction of
  equal standing to Classical Mathematics, with the golden section as its
  axiomatic foundation. Source: Stakhov, "Clarifying the Origins," p. 12,
  http://peacefromharmony.org/docs/7-27_Stakhov_Math_of_Harmony_EN.pdf;
  World Scientific (2009),
  https://www.worldscientific.com/worldscibooks/10.1142/6635. **Reason:**
  (a) No Fpath stated. The claim that Harmony Mathematics is an independent
  mathematical direction with equal standing to the axiomatic tradition is a
  programmatic assertion; no experiment or mathematical construction is
  specified that would falsify it. The existence of interesting phi-based
  theorems is [Verified]; their elevation to a separate foundational
  mathematics is an interpretive move without a stated falsifier.

- **[Risk]** The "Fibonacci computers" universality claim: that Fibonacci
  p-codes constitute an important architectural principle for computer
  arithmetic, with the implication that computing should (or could fruitfully)
  be restructured around Fibonacci number systems. Source: Stakhov,
  "Clarifying the Origins," p. 21-22,
  http://peacefromharmony.org/docs/7-27_Stakhov_Math_of_Harmony_EN.pdf.
  **Reason:** (a) No Fpath demonstrated, and (a)/(d) venue. 65 patents
  confirmed (see [Verified] inventory); the subject describes a project
  developed from 1976 to 1991 in the Soviet Union; no independently
  documented working hardware prototype, measured benchmark comparison, or
  post-1991 physical implementation has been located in a retrievable public
  source. The arithmetic properties of Fibonacci p-codes (redundancy,
  error-detection capacity) are [Verified]; the claim that these make
  "Fibonacci computers" practically competitive with binary architecture is
  [Risk] (a) -- no Fpath stated and no hardware demonstration found.

- **[Risk]** The "Golden Scientific Revolution" framing: that the accumulated
  phi-related discoveries across mathematics, physics, chemistry
  (quasi-crystals, fullerenes), cosmology, and biology constitute a coherent
  scientific revolution driven by the golden section. Source: Stakhov (2016),
  *Computer Science and Cybersecurity*, abstract,
  https://periodicals.karazin.ua/cscs/article/view/6222. **Reason:** (a) and
  (b). No Fpath stated for the claim that phi is the unifying causal thread
  across these domains. Look-elsewhere correction is absent: quasi-crystals
  are explained by Penrose tilings whose relationship to phi is
  crystallographic, not axiomatic; fullerene geometry is icosahedral; each
  domain has a domain-specific non-phi explanation that the "revolution"
  framing does not engage.

- **[Risk]** Bodnar's geometry as a "necessary" geometric explanation of
  phyllotaxis (the stronger reading of the phyllotaxis claim, distinct from
  the [Open conjecture] above). Source: Stakhov & Aranson (2010),
  https://www.scirp.org/pdf/AM20100100019_19605174.pdf. **Reason:** (c) and
  (d). A control programme matches: the Douady-Couder (1992) mechanistic model
  (*Physical Review Letters*, 68, 2098,
  https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.68.2098) derives Fibonacci
  phyllotaxis from a physical self-organisation process -- the golden mean
  arises as a dynamical attractor, not as an input geometry. If the claim is
  "phi-hyperbolic geometry is the necessary geometric origin," the Douady-
  Couder model is a matched control that generates identical observational
  predictions with fewer mathematical assumptions about the geometric
  structure. Venue for the geometric claim is SCIRP only; no independent
  peer-reviewed biology or geometry journal has adjudicated the comparison.

- **[High-risk]** The "Fundamental Suggestion" that the mathematics of harmony
  constitutes "cogent argument of the existence of the Universal Reason, which
  constructs a 'Nature Building' according to the Laws of mathematical beauty."
  Source: Stakhov, "Clarifying the Origins," p. 27,
  http://peacefromharmony.org/docs/7-27_Stakhov_Math_of_Harmony_EN.pdf.
  **Reason:** (a) and (b). The claim is unfalsifiable as stated -- "Universal
  Reason" is not a physical quantity with a measurable signature. This fails
  (a): no Fpath. It also fails (b): look-elsewhere correction is entirely
  absent. The subject's own framing ("Fundamental Suggestion ... if ... then")
  marks this as speculative; we record it as [High-risk] to register both
  failures. This is NOT a character judgment; the claim is classified
  separately from the mathematical results, which are [Verified].

**Prose.** Both programmes share an epistemic structure: a sound algebraic
core ([Verified] identities, reproducible arithmetic) surrounded by a wider
programme that has not supplied an actionable falsification path. Stakhov's
[Verified] layer -- the Fibonacci p-number algebra, Q-matrices, hyperbolic
function identities -- is genuine mathematics. Our [Verified] layer -- the
GF ladder rule 9/9, the Lucas identity -- is genuine arithmetic. The
disagreement between both programmes and the framework arises one level up,
where the algebraic results are treated as evidence for a larger paradigm
claim without a matched-control experiment.

The joint Fpath that would address both programmes simultaneously would be a
pre-registered survey of non-phi number systems with comparable algebraic
depth: does any other irrational base (Tribonacci ratio, square root of 2,
Euler's number) generate an equally rich set of recurrence identities,
hyperbolic function analogues, and error-correcting code properties? If yes,
the paradigm claim ("Harmony Mathematics is special because of phi") is
weakened for both programmes. If the phi-based system has unique properties
not replicable at other bases, the paradigm claim gains evidence. This is a
well-defined mathematical comparison that neither programme has yet published.

## 10. Audit summary

**Strongest part of the programme:** The mathematical core -- Fibonacci
p-numbers, Q-matrices, golden p-proportions as a generalised number system,
symmetric hyperbolic Fibonacci and Lucas functions -- is [Verified] arithmetic.
The 2009 and 2020-2021 World Scientific monographs are the most formally
developed book-length phi-universalism programme in print, and the algebraic
content is internally coherent. The ternary mirror-symmetrical number system
has a calculable redundancy property (28.3%) that is [Verified].

**Weakest claim:** The "Harmony Mathematics as a second mathematics parallel
to classical mathematics" framing, and especially the "Golden Scientific
Revolution" / "Universal Reason" language in the 2016 paper. These are [Risk]
(a) and [High-risk] respectively: no Fpath stated, no look-elsewhere correction,
and the paradigm claim is not operationalised as a testable prediction.

**Single experiment that would move the largest claim:** A systematic
comparison of Fibonacci p-codes against binary and non-phi redundant number
systems (e.g., non-adjacent form, negabinary, balanced ternary) on matched
error-detection tasks in a hardware prototype would either promote the
"Fibonacci computers" claim to [Empirical fit] with a control, or demote it
further. The comparison does not require large hardware; an FPGA or ASIC
implementation of a Fibonacci p-code arithmetic unit benchmarked against a
binary unit on matched word lengths and error-injection tests is in principle
executable.

**Symmetric position of the lab:** The lab's own analogous claim -- that the
GF phi-ladder has a breadth advantage over non-phi float families -- is in the
same pre-falsification state: the falsifier is stated (F2/F3 ablation with
posit/takum control), but the experiment is compute-pending. Both programmes
have the decisive test identified; neither has run it.
