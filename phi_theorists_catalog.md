# Catalog of phi-Theorists: Public Claim-Audit Edition

**Purpose:** A ranked catalog of currently-active (or foundationally-cited) phi-theorists whose published
claims can be audited under the 5-label claim-status framework:
- [Verified arithmetic only]
- [Empirical fit with free params]
- [Open conjecture with Fpath]
- [Risk: no Fpath stated]
- [Fringe-adjacent: cited only by sympathetic outlets]

**Scope:** Persons who assert phi is *fundamental, universal, or explanatory* -- not merely descriptive -- in
physics, biology, cosmology, number systems, consciousness, architecture, economics, ML/AI, or
numerology-adjacent domains.

**Exclusions:** Pure combinatorialists (Knuth, Vajda), historical figures with no living research programme
(Pacioli, Zeising referenced only in passing), anyone without at least one verifiable source URL.

**Note on Scott Olsen:** Identified as a collaborator on the Pellis-Vasilev-Olsen paper and as research-adjacent
to the audit lab. His entry is included with "adjacent" framing; it is not an adversarial audit target.

**Ranking:** Ordered by priority/interest for a public claim-audit, from highest to lowest.

## 02. Mohamed El Naschie

- **Affiliation / venue:** Independent; formerly listed affiliations with University of Cambridge
  (disputed), University of Frankfurt, Alexandria University. Founding editor-in-chief of *Chaos,
  Solitons & Fractals* (Elsevier), 1991-2009.
- **Primary phi-claim(s):** E-infinity theory: quantum spacetime is a random Cantor set with Hausdorff
  dimension 4 + phi^3 = 4.236067..., with the golden ratio appearing as the fundamental organising
  constant of particle physics and cosmology.
- **Domain:** Physics (high-energy, quantum gravity, cosmology); also claimed applications to dark
  energy and the Standard Model coupling constants.
- **Books / key papers:**
  - El Naschie, M.S. (2009). "The theory of Cantorian spacetime and high energy particle physics (an
    informal review)." *Chaos, Solitons and Fractals*, 41, 2635-46.
    [sciencedirect abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0960077906002049)
  - El Naschie, M.S. et al. (2012). "On the Need for Fractal Logic in High Energy Quantum Physics."
    Authors: El Naschie, Olsen, He, Nada, Marek-Crnjac, Helal.
    [scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=23087](https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=23087)
  - El Naschie, M.S. (2013). "The Three Page Guide to the Most Important Results of M. S. El Naschie's
    Research in E-Infinity Quantum Physics and Cosmology." *SCIRP Open Journal of Microphysics*.
    [scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=39841](https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=39841)
- **Citation status:** Publication record concern -- El Naschie published over 300 papers as
  editor-in-chief of *Chaos, Solitons & Fractals*, with 82% self-citations (ISI count 2008). In 2008,
  *Nature* reported on the absence of credible peer review; El Naschie sued for libel. In July 2012 the
  UK court dismissed the case, ruling the *Nature* article was "substantially true" and that El Naschie
  "had failed to provide any documentary evidence that his papers had been peer-reviewed."
  [Wikipedia: Mohamed El Naschie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_El_Naschie);
  [Scholarly Kitchen, 2008](https://scholarlykitchen.sspnet.org/2008/11/25/elsevier-math-editor-controversy/);
  [Backreaction blog, 2008](http://backreaction.blogspot.com/2008/11/chaos-solitons-and-self-promotion.html).
  Post-2010 papers appear in SCIRP journals without mainstream peer review.
- **Tier estimate (preliminary):** [Risk: no Fpath stated] -- The mathematical skeleton of E-infinity
  (Cantor sets, Hausdorff dimensions, Penrose tiling) draws on legitimate mathematics (Mauldin-Williams
  1986 theorem). However, the claimed derivations of Standard Model parameters from phi lack any
  independently checkable path from the mathematical identities to measured quantities; they constitute
  what John Baez (UC Riverside) termed "undisciplined numerology filled with impressive buzzwords."
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_El_Naschie). The publication record concern
  further limits auditability.
- **Why notable for audit-lab:** El Naschie is the highest-visibility case of phi-universalism in
  theoretical physics, with a documented institutional dispute (Elsevier/2008-2009) and a concluded
  court record (2012). This makes him the clearest test case for the Risk tier: the claims are
  formally specific (Hausdorff dimension = 4 + phi^3) but the derivation path to experimental
  predictions has not been independently validated.
- **Verbatim quote (strongest single claim):** "El Naschie's E-Infinity theory has clearly shown that
  random Cantor sets are the basic building blocks of quantum space-time." [Source: SCIRP summary of
  E-infinity results (2013), authored by Marek-Crnjac](https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=39841)

  - Petoukhov, S.V. (2008). arXiv preprint: "Matrix genetics, part 1: permutations of positions in
    triplets and genetic matrices." [arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0888](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0888)
  - Petoukhov, S.V., & He, M. (2010). *Symmetrical Analysis Techniques for Genetic Systems and
    Bioinformatics: Advanced Patterns and Applications.* IGI Global.
  - He, M., & Petoukhov, S.V. (2024). "Hadamard matrices of genetic code and trigonometric functions."
    *Constructive Mathematical Analysis*, 7(Special Issue), 27-36.
    [dergipark.org.tr](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/cma/article/1539666)

  hydrogen-bond matrix identity have unique status among genetic encodings, or is it one of many
  accidental algebraic coincidences? This is a well-defined audit target.

  (2011), "The Genetic Code, the Golden Section and Genetic Music," petoukhov.com]
  (http://petoukhov.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/sciforum-003889-from-PETOUKHOV-.pdf)

  electrostatics. This is in principle falsifiable against quantum electrodynamics calculations.
  The discrepancy between her self-reported "exactness" and the precision of QED is the central
  audit question.

  atom! Thus, the ratio of the electrostatic radii of the electron and proton in the hydrogen atom

  viXra:1603.0199v1, p.4](https://vixra.org/pdf/1603.0199v1.pdf)

  best current example of a phi claim that has moved from [Open conjecture] toward [Empirical fit].
